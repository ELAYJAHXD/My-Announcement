OFFICIAL ANNOUNCEMENT

Suspension of Classes Due to Typhoon Tonidogs

Good day, students, parents, faculty members, and staff of College National High School. Due to the continuous heavy rainfall and strong winds brought by Typhoon Tonidogs, classes in all grade levels are officially suspended tomorrow to ensure the safety and well-being of everyone. This announcement is issued by the Office of the Principal after carefully considering the latest weather updates and recommendations from local authorities.

We encourage everyone to remain indoors whenever possible and avoid unnecessary travel during the bad weather. Students are advised to regularly monitor their official class group chats, school Facebook page, or other communication platforms for announcements regarding make-up classes, online learning activities, and further updates. Parents and guardians are also requested to guide and supervise their children while classes are suspended.

Important Reminders:
  • Always prioritize your safety and avoid flooded areas.
  • Prepare emergency kits, drinking water, flashlights, batteries, and essential medicines.
  • Keep your mobile phones fully charged for emergency communication.
  • Monitor official weather advisories released by local authorities.
  • Wait for the next official announcement regarding the resumption of classes.
  • Students should complete any assigned online or modular activities once it is safe to do so.
